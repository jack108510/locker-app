# Locker App Store Prep

## App identity

- App name: Locker
- Bundle ID: `com.jswenterprises.locker`
- Category: Education
- Age rating direction: 12+ recommended because user-generated school material is uploaded and moderated.

## Subtitle

Your school test bank

## Promotional text

Find old assignments, quizzes, exams, and answer-filled copies from your school. Scan past material, search by class or teacher, and prep with real examples.

## Short description

Locker is a school-specific test-prep database built from old assignments, quizzes, exams, worksheets, and answer-filled copies scanned by students.

## App Store description

Locker helps students prepare for tests using real past school material from their own school.

Scan old assignments, quizzes, exams, worksheets, or answer-filled copies. Locker extracts text from the page, adds class and teacher labels, and makes approved material searchable for students in the same school feed.

What Locker does:

- Search your school’s old assignments, quizzes, exams, and answers
- Scan pages with the camera or choose a photo
- Extract searchable text with OCR
- Filter by material type, class, and teacher
- Report content that should not be public
- Block a source you do not want to see

Locker is for test prep with past material. Current tests, teacher-only keys, personal student information, grades, rosters, and abusive content are not allowed and may be removed.

## Review notes

Locker is a user-generated-content education app. Users choose an alias and school, then see only approved material for that school. The app includes:

- in-app reporting for each submitted material card
- local source blocking
- moderation queue/report records in Supabase
- privacy policy and terms/content policy pages
- prohibited-content copy in the scan flow
- camera/photo permission strings explaining scan usage

Current tests, teacher-only keys, student personal information, and abuse are prohibited.

## Privacy nutrition-label draft

Data collected:

- User Content: photos/scans, OCR text, report content, submitted titles/labels
- Identifiers: alias/handle, local device identifier for duplicate vote prevention
- Other Data: selected school, course/teacher tags, votes/reports

Data not collected:

- real name required by app: no
- email required by app: no
- precise location: no
- contacts: no
- browsing history: no
- health/financial data: no

Purpose:

- App functionality
- Content moderation
- Fraud/abuse prevention
